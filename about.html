About Rabbi Chaim Dalfin

For over 25 years, Rabbi Dalfin has brought the Rebbe's light to audiences worldwide — through farbrengens, books, and stories that turn Chassidus into daily courage.

He is the author of more than 60 books on Lubavitch history, the Rebbe, and Chassidic life. His talks blend personal memory, rigorous research, and emotional storytelling.

RCD.com is the home for his video clips, speaking bookings, and connection to his full catalog at rabbidalfin.com.
